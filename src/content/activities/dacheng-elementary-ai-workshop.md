role: >-
  Joined GDG on Campus NTPU for an AI workshop at Dacheng Elementary School in
  Sanxia, New Taipei. The club president ran the session on stage; I worked
  the floor with other volunteers, guiding kids one-on-one. The task we gave
  them sounds simple: come up with your own prompt, type it to the AI
  yourself, and post your creation to a shared class board.
summary: >-
  What struck me working alongside them: the guidance they needed was never
  "which button to press." It was helping them turn a fuzzy idea in their
  head into one clear instruction. Knowing what you want and saying it
  precisely — that's the first lesson of working with AI, whether you're
  eight or twenty-eight. Thanks to Dacheng Elementary School, the AirTAC–SanMei
  Charity Foundation, and my GDG teammates for making it happen.
