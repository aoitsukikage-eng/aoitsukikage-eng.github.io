# Research publication policy

## Publication boundary

Research is published as complete, source-traceable HTML for direct reading.
Complete report files are not offered for download, redistribution, or
re-upload. Original XLSX and DOCX files are not published. The policy protects
source-file metadata and third-party material while keeping the public research
evidence available on the relevant web page.

The boundary is the file, not the content. Withholding a source file never
implies withholding the analysis it contains: where the underlying material is
already public (statutes, administrative orders, court judgments), the research
argument, method, evidence, and conclusions are published in full on the page.

Author-created charts remain embedded reading assets. They are not presented as
downloadable files.

Author-created diagrams follow the same rule. The SinoPac case study carries
four: the fund-flow and profit-share structure, the two related-party
derivation paths, the three-lines-of-defence failure, and the
administrative-versus-judicial dual-track timeline.

Where a diagram names individuals, it is limited to the relationships that are
legally necessary to explain the statutory test at issue, carries an explicit
notice that no wrongdoing is implied, and states which named individuals are not
defendants. Where criminal proceedings are unresolved, the page carries a
non-final notice at the top and in the publication-boundary section.
