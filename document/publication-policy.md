# Research publication policy

## Publication boundary

Research is published as complete, source-traceable HTML for direct reading.
Complete report files are not offered for download, redistribution, or
re-upload. Original XLSX and DOCX files are not published. The policy protects
source-file metadata and third-party material while keeping the public research
evidence available on the relevant web page.

Author-created charts remain embedded reading assets. They are not presented as
downloadable files.

The review confirms that every public-edition research question, method, core
data, chart explanation, limitation, and source statement is readable in the
corresponding HTML page. No public-edition PDF is part of the publication
surface.
